The Fairport Merchants now have a home online. Everything on the weekly standings sheet is here, plus a few things paper can't do:

- **Standings and results**, updated after each league night.
- **Schedule**, with this week's lanes and every past result. Pick your team to highlight its matches.
- **Bowlers**: choose any bowler to see every game this season, the average he bowled on, points won, who he faced and how he does on each pair.
- **Teams**: week-by-week results, roster and lane-pair numbers.
- **Insights**, the **300 Club** and the **league rules**.

Spot something that doesn't look right? Let the league secretary know.
