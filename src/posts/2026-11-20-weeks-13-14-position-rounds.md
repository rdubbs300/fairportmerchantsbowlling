The last two weeks of the first half are **position rounds**: week 13 on **Thursday, December 3** and week 14 on **Thursday, December 10**. Matchups are set by the standings, 1 v 2, 3 v 4 and so on down the sheet. There is no bowling on Thanksgiving, November 26.

Two league rules apply to both weeks.

**Substitutes.** From the league rules:

> "No new substitutes are allowed on weeks 13, 14, 27, 28 and 29. To bowl those weeks, a substitute must have already bowled 3 games in the league during the current season."

**Pre-bowls.** From the league rules:

> "No pre-bowls are allowed on weeks 13, 14, 27, 28 and 29 unless approved by a league officer or in compliance with USBC rule 117b."

### Who can sub

All 23 substitutes who have bowled this season already have at least 3 games, so any of them may bowl: Brian Barnes, Tom Berardicurti, Jeff Converse, Steve Essler, Rick Evans, Phil Fiorica, Scott Ghidiu, Matt Hallman, Pierce Hawley, Richard Keuer, Chris Lamb, Bill Martens, Aaron Masciango, SJ Morgante, Rich Osinski, Tony Rebis, Patrick Ritz, Zach Roos, Oliver Del Rosario, Nick Ruston, Randy Squier, Jeff Uteg and Jamie Vanarsdall. Anyone else cannot bowl these two weeks.
